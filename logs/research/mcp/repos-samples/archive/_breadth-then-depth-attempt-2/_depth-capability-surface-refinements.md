# Depth Pass Refinements — Sample > Capability surface

Per-role cross-corpus refinement proposals from inspecting every sample's content under this role. Capability surface is the most diverse role in the consolidated tree (~38 paths, 33 with supporting samples, 5 with descriptions but no current samples). Sub-axes are visible at the role level — the path family is an *unordered union* of choices on multiple orthogonal axes (tool count/scope, primitives included, authoring style, gating mechanism, auxiliary content), and many samples land on multiple paths because they make choices on each axis. Reconciler should consider whether to surface that axis structure in the role-level prose.

**Most samples place under 2-4 paths in this role.** Crystaldba-postgres-mcp: Tools-only + Capability gating. Korotovsky-slack: Domain-bundled + Tools+resources + Capability gating. **Tool-count is the most informative scope dimension and has natural anchors.** Across the 70+ samples in this role, the count distribution clusters at: 1-2 (single-generic / single-code-exec), 5-10 (narrow), 12-30 (domain-bundled), 30-100 (domain-wrapper), 250+ (surface-maximizer). Suggestion: in the role-level intro, name the count regime as an axis so the reader can locate samples by tool count.

**"Read/write tool split" and "Capability gating flags > read-only mode" describe complementary halves of the same design choice.** A read/write split (two distinct tools) + a read-only flag that disables one of them = the same shape as a single tool with a `mode=readonly` parameter that errors on writes. The split is *what you call the tools*, the gating is *how the operator gates them*. Most "read/write split" samples (5) also place under `Capability gating flags` (PagerDuty, neondatabase, geropl-linear, mongodb, ghost). This is the multi-axis pattern again — primitives-shape axis + gating-mechanism axis. Reconciler doesn't need to act on this; it's a cross-corpus pattern that confirms the axis framing.

**Token-economy compression vs surface-maximization is a real architectural tension.** Two paths surface different ends: `Tools-heavy domain wrapper` (250+ tools as comprehensive surface) and `Token-economy unified-tool surface` (compressed to 1-2 broad tools). One project (HenkDz-postgresql) explicitly went *from* atomic-many *to* meta-fewer (the `Tool consolidation as design pressure` path is its trace). davinci-resolve ships both modes (`Tool-count modes (compound vs full)` path). The corpus shows authors actively negotiating between these poles — context-window pressure pushes toward consolidation, discoverability pushes toward enumeration. This tension could be surfaced in role-level prose as design context, but no individual path needs revision.
